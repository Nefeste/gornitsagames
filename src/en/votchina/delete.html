How to delete your Votchina profile

In the game

Profile → Manage profile → Delete online profile → type your tag (six characters) → Delete forever. An internet connection is required. Before version 2.3 the button is on the Play online screen.

What is deleted

  • your nickname, tag, rating, friends, invitations and any transfer parcel;
  • your device key stops opening anything.

What remains

Finished online games and tournament results — for your opponents these are their games too — but without your name: they show a “deleted player”. A rated game in progress counts as a resignation, and you withdraw from any tournament in progress. Games and history on your phone stay on your phone.

If you no longer have the phone

Send the developer your tag (six characters — your friends can see it in their list). The profile will be deleted by hand, exactly as the in-game button does. Email for questions and deletion requests: gornitsa.games@gmail.com.
