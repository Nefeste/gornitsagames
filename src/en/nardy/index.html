Long Nardy

A coach-style review of every game, fair dice and ads only between games.

Long nardy is the backgammon cousin played across Russia, the Caucasus and Central Asia: fifteen checkers each, no hitting, and a race home past your opponent's blocks. The game is in development and in Russian first; the screenshots are from the 0.5.0 closed-test build. An English version is planned after the release.

A review of every game

  • After each game the app reviews every move, yours and your opponent's, with the same neural network that plays as the Master.
  • Right on the result screen: how many mistakes and blunders, the costliest one and what the best move was.
  • Luck in numbers, for you and your opponent: was it the dice or you?
  • The review screen: any move on the board with the best move highlighted step by step, what the mistake cost, the luck of the roll and a chart of the game; one tap jumps to the next mistake.
  • Games against the bot, on one phone and online with a friend can all be reviewed (online — once the game is over).
  • Free: the summary, luck and the costliest mistake of your last game. The full review of any game comes with Pro, or for one game by watching an ad.

Fair dice

  • Against the bot and on one phone the dice are rolled the moment you roll them: neither the bot nor the game knows them in advance, and a roll can't be redone by restarting the game.
  • Open dice statistics: how often each face and doubles come up, for you and your opponent separately.
  • Online games with a friend come with a check: after the game your phone recomputes every roll itself. How it works, and how to check a game yourself in the browser, is on the fair dice page (in Russian).

Ads only between games

Never during a game, no banners, at most every other game, and none at all in your first games. One purchase removes them for good.

A strong bot

Four levels, from Beginner to Master; the upper three are one neural network trained on millions of games against itself — the higher the level, the less often it allows itself a weaker move. Against the bot you can take back a move or ask for a hint — such a game simply doesn't count towards your score.

Where it stands

Games against the bot at all four levels, on one phone and online with a friend by code or invite link already work, as do the game review and the luck meter. Next come purchases, a closed test and the RuStore listing.
