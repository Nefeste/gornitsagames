How to delete your Kozyr profile

A profile appears only when you play online — at a table with friends or in a quick match: a nickname, a six-character tag and your quick-match rating. We keep no email or phone number. Games against bots, the Road to the Master and the puzzle of the day need no profile: they are kept on your phone.

In the game

In the menu, open С друзьями (With friends) or Быстрая игра (Quick match). At the bottom of the screen there is a line “Вы — nickname · tag” (“You are …”) and the button Удалить профиль (Delete profile). The game asks once; after Удалить (Delete) the profile is erased at once. An internet connection is required: if the server doesn’t answer, the game says so and the profile stays intact. While a quick match is being searched for, the button is hidden — tap Не искать (Stop searching) first.

What is deleted

  • your nickname, tag and quick-match rating — at once;
  • the fingerprint of your device key: the key is forgotten and stops opening anything;
  • your place in the quick-match queue; your own table that is still gathering is closed, and your seat at someone else’s gathering table is freed;
  • a game with friends in progress is finished by a bot in your seat — the others see “удалённый игрок” (“deleted player”) and “играет бот” (“a bot is playing”) there; a quick match in progress counts as your defeat.

What remains

A nameless row stays on the server: a number and two dates — when the profile was created and when you were last in contact. Finished online games are kept for up to 30 days after they end, as for everyone — for your opponents these are their games too — but your seat in them has no name. The daily statistics don’t know who you are: they hold a random installation number not linked to your nickname or profile, so deleting the profile doesn’t affect them; the number is deleted if no statistics arrive for 90 days.

On your phone the game forgets the key, the nickname and the tag. Games against bots, the Road to the Master, your statistics and the “Мне есть 14 лет” (“I am 14 or older”) confirmation stay on your phone and in its Android backup, if backup is turned on. Purchases stay too: RuStore remembers them.

Server request logs are kept for 3 days. Database backups, encrypted ones included, still hold the nickname until they expire — for no longer than 28 days, so after four weeks the profile is gone from them too.

If you no longer have the phone

Send us your nickname and tag (six characters; the game shows them at the bottom of the С друзьями (With friends) and Быстрая игра (Quick match) screens). The profile will be deleted by hand within a week, exactly as the in-game button does. So that a typo in the tag can’t erase someone else’s profile, it is deleted only if the nickname in your email matches the nickname of the profile with that tag. Email for questions and deletion requests: support@gornitsa.games.
