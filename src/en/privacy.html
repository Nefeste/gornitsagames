Privacy policy

How Gornitsa’s games and website handle your data. We collect only what a game can’t work without, and we don’t sell data to third parties.

Version of October 4, 2026. The Russian version is the authoritative one.

1. Who we are

This policy covers the gornitsa.games website and all mobile games by the Gornitsa studio (“we”).

For any questions about your data, write to privacy@gornitsa.games.

These totals contain no IP addresses, browsers or any other details about visitors.

3. Votchina

Votchina is a free game with no ads. It contains no analytics, no ad networks and no third-party tracking. This section has been in effect since September 25, 2026.

Playing on your phone

Games against bots and pass-and-play games send nothing anywhere. A game by link goes only where you send the link yourself — it never passes through our server. Settings, saved games and history stay on your phone.

Playing in a browser

The web version (/play/) works the same way: whatever the phone keeps in its own storage, the browser keeps in its storage (localStorage), and none of it goes to the server. There are no cookies. A game-by-link address carries the move after the “#” sign — the browser never sends that part of an address to the server. If you clear the site data in your browser, the key of your online profile goes with it; you can move your profile beforehand under Profile → Manage profile.

Playing online: what the server keeps

  • The nickname you chose and a six-character tag that friends use to add you.
  • A fingerprint of your device key. The server issues the key on first use; the key itself is stored only on your phone, and the server keeps only its SHA-256 hash. The game never asks for an email, phone number or password.
  • Online games: who is at the table, the rules, the moves, the result, chat messages (emoji and ready-made phrases only — there is no free text), and the tournament clock.
  • Your rating and tournament game results; the tournaments you take part in.
  • Friends and table invitations.
  • When you were last online, to the nearest hour.
  • A profile transfer parcel — your game history and games by link — if you move your profile to another phone. It is kept only while the transfer code is valid (15 minutes at most) and erased when claimed, cancelled or replaced.

What the game does not collect

Advertising ID, location, contacts, photos, files, camera or microphone, or information about other apps. Turn notifications are local: your phone asks the server itself, and the server stores no push tokens.

Problem reports

Settings → Report a problem builds a report: the game version, your phone model and Android version, a log of recent crashes (kept only on your phone) and, if you leave the box checked, a link to the open game (it contains player names) or the online game’s number. You see the whole text before sending. The report is sent only when you tap Send (Copy in a browser) — with your email app or a messenger of your choice; the game itself never sends it anywhere.

Who can see it

Your nickname, tag and rating are visible to your opponents and friends; a game is visible to the players at the table and to members of the same tournament. Data is not shared with or sold to anyone else. The game server runs in Russia and stores the data there; request logs (including IP addresses) are kept for three days.

How long it is kept and how to delete it

For as long as your profile exists. Results of finished games remain after you delete your profile — for your opponents these are their games too — but without your name. You can delete your profile in the game: Profile → Manage profile → Delete online profile (before version 2.3: Play online → Delete profile). If you no longer have the phone, see how to delete your profile.

Age

The game is intended for everyone aged 13 and over; online play is not directed at children.

4. Skazy

Skazy is a game with no ads: the beginning is free, and the rest of the book and the heroes’ folk paintings can be bought. It works offline; the network is needed only for the outpost — playing with friends. The game contains no analytics, no ad networks and no third-party tracking. The game is being prepared for release in RuStore; this section already applies to the closed-test versions.

Playing on your phone

Your household — the band, items, silver, the versts you have travelled — and your settings are kept only on your phone and are deleted together with the game. Until you join an outpost or open an invitation to one, the game contacts the studio’s server only once a day, to send the daily statistics, which contain no data about you (see below).

Save to file in Settings packs the game into a file and hands it to the system Share menu — the file goes only where you send it yourself. Load from file opens the system file picker; the game reads only the file you choose.

The outpost: what the server keeps

The outpost is optional and open from the age of 14 (see Age below). When you join it for the first time, the studio’s server issues your phone a random key, and you choose a nickname. The game never asks for an email, phone number or password. The server keeps only what the outpost can’t work without:

  • Your nickname and six-character tag, by which your comrades recognise you, and a fingerprint of your device key (a SHA-256 hash); the key itself is not on the server.
  • The outpost: which outpost you are in, its name, invitation code and leader.
  • The outpost feed — ready-made phrases and emoji only; there is no free text in the game. Entries are kept for no longer than 30 days.
  • A snapshot of the helper hero you lent to your comrades.
  • The weekly hunt — the monster, its strength, its health and the damage dealt by the whole outpost — and your strikes: the day, the damage and a fingerprint of the battle log the server used to verify the strike. The server replays the strike’s line-up and doesn’t keep it. Hunts and strikes are kept for five weeks.
  • Gifts and “thank you” notes for your helper until you open them: a gift for up to three days, everything else for up to 30 days.
  • The day of your last visit, without the time: your comrades see “played today”.
  • A copy of your household — the latest and the previous one: it lets you move the game to another phone and not lose it together with the phone.
  • A transfer code — for 15 minutes; command replies — for a day, so that a retry over a poor connection isn’t carried out twice.

There are no IP addresses in the database.

Who can see it

Your nickname, tag, helper, feed phrases and the day of your last visit are visible to your comrades in the outpost. The outpost’s name, its leader’s nickname and the number of members are visible to anyone who has the invitation code or link. Data is not shared with or sold to anyone else. The outpost server runs in Russia, on the same machine as the gornitsa.games website; its request logs are kept for 3 days, and backups of its database for no longer than 28 days (see section 8).

How long it is kept and how to delete it

For as long as your profile exists; a profile that hasn’t been used for 18 months is deleted together with its copies. You can delete your profile in the game: Settings → Profile → Delete profile. The server erases your nickname, your place in the outpost, your feed entries, helper, parcels, hunt strikes and copies; the damage you dealt stays in the outpost’s total without your name, and the game on your phone stays. If you no longer have the phone, see how to delete your profile.

Moving to another phone

With a one-time code valid for 15 minutes: your profile and the latest copy of your household move to the new phone, and the old one carries on without a profile.

Daily statistics

Once a day the game sends the studio’s server the day’s totals — without your nickname, profile, key or any phone or installation number: the install date, the day, the game version and store, the number of visits and minutes, how many visits ended in a crash (the game closed by itself or showed “Something went wrong”), the farthest verst, the number of completed tales, whether Today’s tasks were done, whether you have the Whole Book, and whether you are in an outpost. The server immediately adds them to the day’s totals and doesn’t keep the submission itself. We use these totals to decide what to improve in the game.

Notifications

Evening Tale is a single notification in the evening, at a time you choose, when the band has returned from a journey or the travel bag is almost full. The game asks for permission once; without it there are no notifications. The notification is scheduled by the phone itself — it isn’t a push notification: it needs no server, and the server knows nothing about it. You can turn it off in the game’s settings or in the phone’s settings.

Permissions

Internet and network state (the outpost, daily statistics and purchases), vibration, audio settings, notifications (Evening Tale) and starting after the phone restarts, so that a scheduled notification isn’t lost. The game asks for no other permissions.

What the game does not collect

Advertising ID, location, contacts, photos or files (other than the save file you choose), camera or microphone, or information about other apps.

Problem reports

Report a problem — in Settings and on the “Something went wrong” screen — opens your email app with a message to support@gornitsa.games containing the game version, your phone model and Android version, and the game’s recent errors from its crash log (kept only on your phone). You see the whole message, and it is sent only if you send it yourself.

Purchases

The Whole Book and the paintings are bought through RuStore Pay. We never see or store card or payment details, and purchases never reach our server: what you have bought is known to RuStore and your phone. When the game opens, it asks RuStore what you have bought — that’s how purchases come back on a new phone. RuStore Pay itself sends RuStore the details needed for payment and technical details of how payment works, under RuStore’s rules; we don’t receive them.

Age

The game is intended for everyone aged 6 and over. In RuStore’s kids mode, a parent confirms purchases. The outpost — playing with other people through the studio’s server — is open from the age of 14: before you create a profile, the game asks you to confirm “I am 14 or older”. The single-player game sends no data about you and is open to everyone.

6. Legal grounds

We process data to perform the user agreement you enter into when you install and launch a game, and on the basis of your consent.

7. Who we share data with

  • RuStore — distributing our games and handling payments and subscriptions. In games with purchases, the RuStore Pay payment SDK itself sends RuStore details of payments and of how payment works, under RuStore’s rules; we don’t receive them.
  • A hosting provider in Russia — the gornitsa.games website, Votchina’s online mode and the Skazy outpost run on its server.

We don’t sell or rent out data. Our released games have no third-party analytics and no ad networks; if a game gets them, its section will say so before release.

8. Where and how long we keep data

The gornitsa.games website, Votchina’s online mode and the Skazy outpost run on a server in Russia, and the data is stored there. Encrypted backups of the databases are also kept on the studio’s computer, likewise in Russia.

  • A Votchina online profile — for as long as it exists (see section 3).
  • A Skazy outpost profile — for as long as it exists; a profile that hasn’t been used for 18 months is deleted together with its copies (see section 4).
  • Game progress is stored on your phone and purchases in RuStore; games in development will get their own section before release.
  • Support correspondence — 1 year after the last email.
  • Website server logs — no longer than 30 days; anonymous visit totals — 1 year.
  • Votchina and Skazy server request logs — 3 days.
  • Backups of the server databases and of support correspondence — 7 days on the server itself; encrypted backups — 14 days on the server and no longer than 14 days on the studio’s computer. Only the studio can decrypt them.

After these periods, data is deleted or anonymized. Deleted data also disappears from backups within 28 days.

9. Children

We don’t knowingly collect personal data from children under 14 without parental consent. If you are a parent and believe your child has given us data, write to us and we will delete it.

10. Your rights

At any time you can:

  • find out what data we hold about you and how we use it;
  • ask us to correct inaccurate data;
  • ask us to delete your data or account;
  • withdraw your consent to data processing;
  • complain to Roskomnadzor if you believe we are violating your rights.

To exercise these rights, write to privacy@gornitsa.games from the address or account you use in the game; for Votchina, include your tag; for Skazy, your nickname and tag. We reply within 10 business days and delete data within 30 days of the request.

11. How we protect data

Data is transmitted over a secure connection (HTTPS), only the studio’s developers have access to the servers, and passwords and keys are stored separately from the code.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change the policy, the new version will appear on this page with a new date. We will announce significant changes in the game.
