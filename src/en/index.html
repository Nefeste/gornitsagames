Long Nardy

Classic long nardy (long backgammon) under the official rules of Russia’s Ministry of Sport. Play a bot at four levels, two players on one phone, or online with a friend by code or link — no sign-up.

Anamnesis

Patients come to you with real illnesses and injuries, and almost every sign stays hidden until you ask, examine or order a test. Make a diagnosis, choose a treatment and see the outcome — and between shifts, build your own hospital.

How we make games

Four studio rules

We read about two thousand reviews of games on RuStore. People complain about the same few things again and again, and you won’t find them in our games.

Fair randomness

Tiles and dice favor neither the bot nor paying players. In Long Nardy each online roll is built from parts supplied by the server and both phones, and you can verify it after the game.

Play without a connection

Games against bots and two players on one phone work offline. To play a friend by correspondence, a link in a messenger is all you need.

No sign-up

A nickname is all you need to play online: we never ask for an email, a phone number or a password.

Ads don’t get in the way

Votchina, Anamnesis, Skazy and Uzory have no ads at all. In Long Nardy they will appear only between games, and a single purchase turns them off for good.

“A house is beautiful not for its corners, but for its pies.” A Russian proverb we keep in mind when deciding what to add to a game

About the studio

Gornitsa is a small independent studio. We make games that parents, grandparents and friends enjoy after work: clear rules, big buttons, an unhurried pace.

We release every game on RuStore first and support it ourselves: we answer emails, read reviews and fix whatever gets in the way.
