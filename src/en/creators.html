Creators and streamers

Record our games

Make let’s plays, reviews and streams of any Gornitsa game — there’s no need to ask permission. We’d be glad to see what you make.

What you can do

  • Record and stream any of our games, in full or in part, with the game’s sound.
  • Earn from your videos: platform ads, viewer donations, paid channel memberships.
  • Use game banners and icons from the Press page, the studio mark from the brand book and our wallpapers for covers and descriptions.
  • Criticise a game if it deserves it. An honest review helps us more than praise.

What we ask

  • Put a link to the game in the description — short addresses are below.
  • Don’t present your channel or video as the studio’s official channel, and don’t use the Gornitsa mark as your avatar.
  • Don’t upload game files or modified versions.

Links for your description

A short link leads to the game on RuStore, or to its page on our site while the game isn’t there yet.

Questions

The platform flagged my video for copyright

Write to press@gornitsa.games with a link to the video and the text of the claim. We’ll confirm by email that we allow the video and look into what caused the flag.

Can you give a promo code or a gift for my viewers?

No: the studio has no prizes or gifts for anyone. Money can’t buy an advantage in our games, and giving one away would be unfair to the other players too.

Can I ask questions about a game or do an interview?

Yes. Tell us which game and which channel the video is for — we’ll answer and send screenshots if you need them.

Email for creators

Send us a link to your video or stream, or ask about a game — we’ll reply. It’s the same address as for the press.
