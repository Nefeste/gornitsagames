Play our games before anyone else

Before a game reaches RuStore, and before big updates, we give it to a small group of people. They play as usual and tell us what gets in the way — and the game ships with their fixes in it. Join them.

Join the test

What you get

  • Games and updates before anyone else. The test version installs from RuStore like any other game and updates there too.
  • A direct line to the people who make the game. Every email gets a real answer: what it was and when we’ll fix it.
  • A game with your fix in it. What you find, we fix before release — other players won’t run into it. We write about what changed after a test in the news and the studio channels.

What we need from you

  • An Android phone with the RuStore app. Affordable and older phones are especially welcome: our games are made for them too.
  • Play as you normally would, at your own pace — as much as you like.
  • Tell us what broke or got in the way: with “Report a problem” in the game’s settings, or by email.

How to join

Write four lines to qa@gornitsa.games:

  1. which game you’d like to play — or “any”;
  2. your phone model;
  3. your Android version;
  4. your VK ID.

When a test starts, we’ll add you to the tester list on RuStore and email you a link to the test version.

Why VK ID, and where to find it

RuStore opens a closed test only to people the developer has added to a list, and the list is built from VK IDs — the account you sign in to RuStore with. Without it the store won’t show you the test version.

A VK ID is a number. You can find it in your account settings at id.vk.com; for a VK page it is in the address: vk.com/id followed by digits. Can’t find it? Send a link to your VK page and we’ll help. Make sure RuStore on your phone is signed in with the same account.

A four-line request

Fill it in and we’ll put the email together. It opens in your mail app and you send it yourself: the site stores nothing and sends nothing anywhere.

We use your email and VK ID only to invite you to a test and to reply to you: we enter the VK ID into the game’s tester list in the RuStore console and remove it when the test ends or when you ask. No name or phone number needed. The correspondence is kept for a year after the last email and deleted sooner if you ask. Details are in the privacy policy.
