Become a tester

Before a game reaches RuStore, and before big updates, we give it to a small group of people. They play as usual and tell us what gets in the way. Join them.

What we need from you

  • An Android phone. Affordable and older phones are especially welcome: our games are made for them too.
  • Play as you normally would, at your own pace. There are no special tasks.
  • Tell us what broke or got in the way: with “Report a problem” in the game’s settings, or by email.

What you get

  • New games and updates before anyone else.
  • A reply to every email, and fixes before release — what you found, other players won’t run into.

There are no rewards or prizes for testing: the studio has none for anyone. The games in development are on the home page.

How to join

Write to qa@gornitsa.games: which game you’d like to play (or “any”), your phone model and Android version. No name or phone number needed. When a test starts, we’ll email you how to install the test version.

We use your email only to invite you to a test and to reply to you; the correspondence is kept for a year after the last email and deleted sooner if you ask. Details are in the privacy policy.
