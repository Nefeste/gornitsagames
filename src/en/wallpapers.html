How to set them

  • Phone. Download the image, open it in your gallery and choose “Set as wallpaper” from the menu; the wording differs a little between phones. The mark sits above the middle so the icons at the bottom of the screen don’t cover it.
  • Computer. The image is made for a 4K screen and scales down cleanly. On Windows, right-click it and choose “Set as desktop background”; on macOS, go to System Settings → Wallpaper.

What you can do with them

Use them on your own phones and computers and share a link to this page. Printing the mark or the patterns on merchandise needs our consent: write to hello@gornitsa.games. The mark in SVG and how to handle it are in the brand book.
