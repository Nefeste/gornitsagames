Wallpapers

Embroidery for your screen

Four embroideries for phones and computers, each in a light and a dark version. The whole cloth is canvas: one cell size, two threads in every stitch, the top one lying over the bottom one. Drawn by code, no text. Free to download.

Linen

The studio mark inside a large lozenge of flax thread, with spruce crosses at the corners; on the computer the thread runs from the lozenge to the edges of the screen, like a towel through a medallion. The dark version is called Evening.

Light: phone, 1440 × 3200 computer, 3840 × 2160

Dark (Evening): phone, 1440 × 3200 computer, 3840 × 2160

Rushnik

A towel across the screen: zigzags, the band from our site and a chain of rhombs, with the mark in the middle of the chain in place of a rhomb. Works well on a lock screen too: the clock stays on plain linen.

Light: phone, 1440 × 3200 computer, 3840 × 2160

Dark (Rushnik at night): phone, 1440 × 3200 computer, 3840 × 2160

Canvas

The pattern from our home page, stitched large in three threads inside a zigzag frame; on the computer the frame is wider, with chain rhombs on either side.

Light: phone, 1440 × 3200 computer, 3840 × 2160

Dark (Canvas at night): phone, 1440 × 3200 computer, 3840 × 2160

Tablecloth

A lattice of rhombs in flax thread over the whole cloth, with spruce crosses at the knots and a single red stitch in every rhomb. The calmest of the set: phone icons stay readable on top.

Light: phone, 1440 × 3200 computer, 3840 × 2160

Dark (Tablecloth at night): phone, 1440 × 3200 computer, 3840 × 2160

How to set them

  • Phone. Download the image, open it in your gallery and choose “Set as wallpaper” from the menu; the wording differs a little between phones. The main part of each picture sits in the middle of the screen: the top is left for the clock and status bar, the bottom for icons and the home bar.
  • Computer. The image is drawn at 4K size, every stitch crisp, and scales down cleanly to 2560 × 1440 and 1920 × 1080; the bottom of the screen is left for the taskbar. On Windows, right-click it and choose “Set as desktop background”; on macOS, go to System Settings → Wallpaper.

What you can do with them

Use them on your own phones and computers and share a link to this page. Printing the mark or the patterns on merchandise needs our consent: write to hello@gornitsa.games. The mark in SVG and how to handle it are in the brand book.
