How to delete your Skazy profile

A profile appears only when you join an outpost: a nickname and a six-character tag. The single-player game needs no profile — it lives on your phone.

In the game

Settings → Profile → Delete profile → Delete profile once more. An internet connection is required.

What is deleted

  • your nickname, tag and place in the outpost: if you are the leader, the member who has been in the outpost longest becomes the leader, and if you are the last member, the outpost closes;
  • your posts in the outpost feed, your helper hero, gifts and parcels;
  • the copy of your game on the server and any transfer codes;
  • your device key stops opening anything.

What remains

The damage you dealt to this week’s hunt stays in the outpost’s total, without your name. The daily statistics never knew who you were: they hold only daily sums. The game on your phone stays on your phone. Server request logs are kept for 3 days and database backups, encrypted ones included, for no longer than 28 days, so after four weeks the profile is gone from them too.

If a profile is not used for 18 months, the server deletes it on its own.

If you no longer have the phone

Send us your nickname and tag (six characters — your outpost friends can see it in their list). The profile will be deleted by hand within a week, exactly as the in-game button does. Email for questions and deletion requests: support@gornitsa.games.
