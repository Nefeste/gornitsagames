---
The studio now has its own [Telegram channel](https://t.me/gornitsa_games) and
[VK community](https://vk.com/gornitsa_games), both in Russian: news about our games and notes on
how we make them — where the rules come from, how we draw with code and why a game leaves
something out.

Both get the same posts, so pick whichever suits you. Questions about purchases,
profiles and data are still best sent to support@gornitsa.games: please don’t leave your profile tag
or order number in comments.
