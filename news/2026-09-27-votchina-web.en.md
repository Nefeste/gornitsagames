title: Votchina in the browser, nothing to install
---
You can play Votchina in the browser on a computer or a phone without installing anything:
[votchina.gornitsa.games/play](https://votchina.gornitsa.games/play/). On Android, get it
from [RuStore](https://www.rustore.ru/catalog/app/com.votchina.game).

More about the game is on [its page](https://gornitsa.games/en/votchina/), and tournament rules are
in [How to run tournaments](https://gornitsa.games/en/votchina/tournaments.html).
