title: The sixth game on the list is Durak
---
We have picked the next game: Durak, the podkidnoy and perevodnoy variants. Its card with the
“Planned” label is on the home page; the title is a working one for now.

Why this one. Durak is the most sought-after tabletop game on RuStore: by our count last
autumn, over a hundred thousand downloads a month, twice as many as backgammon. Half of those
downloads go to copies of other people’s games, and the most visible home-grown one sits at
just over three stars with the same complaints we read about backgammon: a mandatory social
login, crashes, and “the cards aren’t dealt at random”. That is exactly what we promise to do
differently.

What we promise in Durak: the deck is shuffled fairly, and after the game you can check it —
just like the dice rolls in Long Nardy; against the bot or two on one phone the game works
offline; with friends — by link, with no sign-up; ads only between games, removed with a
single purchase, forever. No “coins”, no stakes, no buying strength.

When. We are already building the online engine, the server and the game review for Long
Nardy, so Durak will go into the queue right after it. We’ll name a date when we are sure of it.
