The games we are working on now have their own pages: [Long Nardy](https://gornitsa.games/en/nardy/),
the doctor simulator [Anamnesis](https://gornitsa.games/en/anamnez/), the Russian fairy-tale
role-playing game [Skazy](https://gornitsa.games/en/skazy/) and cross-stitch by numbers
[Uzory](https://gornitsa.games/en/uzory/).

Each page tells what the game is about and how it is different, with screenshots from current
builds. The names are working titles, and we won’t name release dates until we are sure of them.
